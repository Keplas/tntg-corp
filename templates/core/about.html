About T&TG

Tom & The Group
Trade Corporation

Founded on August 26, 2026, T&TG Trade Corporation is a registered Import and Export corporation headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. We operate a Trade and e-Commerce platform designed to facilitate seamless domestic and international commerce across six countries.

Our mission is to bridge the global coffee supply chain by connecting premium Ugandan and Kenyan Arabica and Robusta green coffee with buyers, roasters and distributors across Canada, the United States, Netherlands and Japan. Every transaction on our platform is traceable, accountable and fairly priced.

What We Do

Trade and e-Commerce Platform

T&TG provides a fully integrated trade platform combining an online coffee shop, loyalty rewards, live forex rates and international B2B trade operations.

T&TG Shopping Platform

Premium Arabica and Robusta green coffee sourced from Uganda and Kenya, available to buyers across all six operating countries. Full cart, checkout and order management.

T&TG Loyalty Platform

Earn 0.5% T&TG Loyalty Points on every purchase and 1% on every successful referral. Points are paid out on Day 45. Four pillars: Points, Promotions, Referral and Reward.

Live Exchange Rates

Real-time forex rates for our active trade routes including CAD to UGX, CAD to KES, USD to UGX, EUR to UGX and JPY to UGX. Supporting transparent cross-border pricing.

B2B Trade Operations

Formal B2B trade agreements for coffee suppliers, distributors, registered farms, transportation companies and artisanal manufacturers across all operating regions.

Our Commitment

Traceability, Accountability and Fair Pricing

Every transaction on the T&TG platform is fully documented and traceable from the source farm in Uganda or Kenya to the final buyer in Canada, USA, Netherlands or Japan.

We operate under Ontario law and comply with CFIA regulations, Uganda Coffee Development Authority standards and relevant import regulations in all six operating countries.
