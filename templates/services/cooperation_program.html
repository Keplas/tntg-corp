How the Program Works

Four Steps to Partnership
1
Submit Your Application
Complete the B2B Trade Application Form with your business details, trade direction and product specifications. The application is free.
2
Review Within 2 Business Days
Tom's team reviews your application and contacts you directly to discuss terms, volumes and timeline.
3
Sign the Cooperation Agreement
A formal B2B Seller and Buyer Agreement is issued, governed by Ontario law and compliant with CFIA, UCDA and relevant local regulations in all operating countries.
4
Begin Trade Operations
Your first shipment is coordinated with full documentation, cargo tracking and payment via your preferred method. Earn T&TG Loyalty Points on every transaction.
