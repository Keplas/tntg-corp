The Onboarding Journey

The 4-Stage Mentorship Funnel

Your enrolment journey is built like an application to a prestigious fellowship, not a corporate vendor portal.

01
Stage One
The Discovery Pitch
A short, friendly intake form about your background, your farm/product/digital goals, and why you want to join the network.
02
Stage Two
Cohort Interview
A 15-minute "Strategy & Fit Session" with a T&TG Regional Manager to map out your goals and assign your Growth Coach.
03
Stage Three
The Sandbox / Lab
Access your Student Dashboard to upload company details and seller agreements as "Programme Prerequisites" — not legal compliance.
Stage Four
Launch & Scale
Go live on T&TG's platform with weekly or monthly check-ins with your designated T&TG mentor. Your enterprise, our network.
