B2B vs Franchise

Authorized Reseller — Not a Franchise

Unlike franchisees who operate under our brand's full playbook, B2B Authorized Resellers are independent businesses. You buy our products at wholesale, stock your own shelves, and sell to your customers at the price you decide.

You carry the T&TG "Authorized Reseller" mark, use our product literature and brand assets, and get a personal referral portal so online sales you generate are credited back to you.

  • Your shop, your brand, your pricing — no franchise agreement
  • Buy only what you need — no forced minimum orders once approved
  • Earn referral commission on orders placed through your unique link
  • Display the official T&TG Authorized Reseller badge
